Everything you listen to, and a bit more

The music is the same, streamed from Yandex. The app is different: native, fast, with an equalizer, themes and plugins.

My Wave that adapts to you

An endless stream tuned by activity, character, mood and language. Like and dislike change the wave right away. A live spectrum surrounds the cover, and the screen takes on the cover's color.

Full-screen player with synced lyrics

Lines light up in time with the song; click a line to seek to it. When Yandex has no lyrics, Plastinka looks them up on LRCLib.

Your library

Liked tracks with instant filtering, playlists, albums, artists with their monthly top, listening history and search across the whole catalog.

12 themes, dark and light, any accent

Every theme comes in dark and light variants and can follow the system. Pick one of eight accent colors or your own. Interface scale from 75 to 200 %.

10-band equalizer

From 32 Hz to 16 kHz, 13 presets plus your own, automatic preamp against clipping. Loudness normalization and lossless FLAC quality.

Plugins, no restart needed

FckCensor restores uncensored titles, covers and audio. Slopless labels and skips AI-generated tracks. VibeColorizer tints the wave by the cover, CopyTrackName copies the track name, YandexMusicTime tracks your listening time.

Listen Together

Your PC and phone play the same track from the same second. Only encrypted commands travel between devices.

Discord status

What you're listening to, with the cover, a timer and a "Listen" button.

Mini player

Always on top. Plus media keys and the system media controls: Windows and MPRIS on Linux.

Copies of unavailable tracks

If a track is removed or blocked in your region, Plastinka finds it in another release and plays it.

Android

A separate Kotlin app: My Wave, library, search, lyrics, equalizer, plugins.

Tray and auto-update

Autostart, minimize to tray and updates from GitHub releases — opt-in, in the settings.

Linux: any distro

One build, no deb or rpm needed. Runs on Arch, CachyOS, Manjaro, Fedora 36+, openSUSE, Ubuntu 22.04+ and Debian 12+, on Wayland and X11.

It installs to ~/.local/bin and shows up in the app menu. Media keys and player widgets work via MPRIS; the login is kept in Secret Service (gnome-keyring, KWallet or KeePassXC).

FAQ

Is it safe? Where does my token go?

You sign in with Yandex ID using a device code, so the app never sees your password. The token is stored only in the OS keychain (Windows Credential Manager, Secret Service, Keychain, Android Keystore) and is sent only to Yandex servers. The code is open and builds are made by GitHub Actions.

Do I need a subscription?

Yes. Plastinka checks for Yandex Plus after sign-in; without it, music doesn't play. It's a client for people who already pay for Yandex Music and want a better app.

Is the interface available in English?

Not yet: the interface is in Russian, like the service itself. Issues and pull requests in English are welcome.

Can my account get banned?

Plastinka talks to the same API endpoints as the official apps. We know of no bans, but we can't give guarantees: it may go against Yandex's terms.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

SmartScreen shows this for any app without a paid code-signing certificate. Click "More info" → "Run anyway". The file is built by GitHub Actions from the public repository, and the checksum is in the release.
